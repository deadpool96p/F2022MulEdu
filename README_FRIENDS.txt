       FIFA 22 CLEAN-ROOM MULTIPLAYER - FRIEND CLIENT SETUP GUIDE
========================================================================

How to join and play with the host in FIFA 22:

STEP 1: EXTRACT FILES
  Copy all files from this ZIP directly into your FIFA 22 installation folder:
  (Where FIFA22.exe is located, e.g. G:\Games\FIFA 22 or C:\...\FIFA 22).

STEP 2: CONNECT TO RADMIN VPN
  1. Open Radmin VPN (or your network VPN).
  2. Join the host's Radmin VPN network.
  3. Copy the host's Radmin VPN IP address.

STEP 3: LAUNCH
  1. Make sure Steam is running in the background.
  2. Double-click "start_client.bat".
  3. When prompted, paste the host's Radmin VPN IP (or press Enter to keep default).
  4. Your Steam persona name will be automatically synchronized.
  5. FIFA 22 will launch with multiplayer active!

MATCHMAKING:
  - In FIFA 22, go to PLAY > QUICKPLAY MODES > ONLINE FRIENDLIES.
  - Or use the Companion UI / In-game overlay to enter the Room Code!
